## 1. Core Premise

There was no ancient civilization.
There were no mystical Anchors.

There was an experiment.

The planet (working name TBD) was deliberately partitioned into one hundred parallel islands.

Each island:
- Is deterministically generated from a seed
- Contains 300 species (Neikos)
- Contains a League structure
- Contains controlled ecological variance
- Believes itself to be the entire world

The inhabitants do not know they are part of a system.
The player does not know they are part of a system.

This is not mythology.
This is containment.

Think: Silo. But horizontal.

## 12. The Experiment — Specific Canon

The experiment is called: **PROJECT HUNDRED.**

It was initiated by an organization known as: **THE CARTOGRAPHERS.**

Publicly, The Cartographers were climate systems architects.
Privately, they believed civilization could not survive planetary-scale collapse.

Their thesis: A single civilization always centralizes and fails. Fragmentation may preserve adaptability.

They created one hundred controlled ecological partitions.
Not time travel. Not magic. Engineered spatial isolation.

Each island is a closed adaptive simulation built from:
- Cloned ecological baselines
- Behavioral seeding algorithms
- Genetic synthesis organisms (Neikos)

---

## 13. The Neikos — What They Are

Neikos are not animals. They are adaptive bio-synthetic entities.

Originally designed as:
- Ecological regulators
- Data collectors
- Stress-response amplifiers

Each Neiko genome contains:
- Environmental adaptation logic
- Behavioral mirroring response to human handlers
- Evolutionary drift markers

They are both companions and instruments.

When a Neiko evolves, the island logs shift.

The League system was introduced to accelerate Neiko-human bonding and stress exposure.

**Battles are not entertainment. They are stress tests.**

## 14. The Founders (Named)

The original Cartographers:

- **Dr. Elian Voss** — Systems Architect (believed fragmentation preserves survival)
- **Dr. Mara Ilyanova** — Bioengineer (designed Neiko genome cores)
- **Director Hale Kincaid** — Funding authority, military liaison

Conflict emerged when Mara discovered PROJECT HUNDRED was not temporary containment — it was permanent isolation.

She attempted to leak internal data. Hale suppressed the leak. Elian allowed the final launch sequence anyway.

Only one of them survived long enough to see activation.
**Which one survives is seed-variable myth.**

## 21. Tiered Containment Architecture (All Five Tiers)

PROJECT HUNDRED is not flat. It is tiered.

Tier is not publicly known. Tier is never labeled in-game. Tier is inferred through systemic intensity.

### Tier I — Baseline Containment
**Purpose:** Measure natural competitive hierarchy under stable conditions.

Characteristics:
- League culturally dominant and respected
- Anomalies rare and subtle
- Neiko mutation rate low
- Relay Nodes difficult to access
- NPCs confident in world stability

Player Endings: The Model Subject / The Efficient Champion / The Silent Wanderer

Transfer Rule: Stabilizing endings remain Tier I. Mild destabilizers escalate to Tier II.

### Tier II — Instability Stress Layer
**Purpose:** Observe adaptation under increased systemic variance.

Characteristics:
- Mutation rates slightly elevated
- League factions show internal disagreement
- Relay Nodes more accessible
- Environmental audio distortions subtle but noticeable

Player Endings: The Adaptive Outlier / The Controlled Destabilizer / The Competitive Extremist / The Ecological Strain Inducer

Transfer Rule: High destabilization → Tier III. Stabilizers → Remain Tier II. Extreme dominance without curiosity → Regression to Tier I.

### Tier III — Observer Escalation Layer
**Purpose:** Study subjects aware of systemic seams.

Characteristics:
- NPC dialogue slips increase
- Certain Neikos show anomaly resonance
- Relay Nodes partially active
- Statistical improbabilities frequent
- League authority questioned in some regions

Player Endings: The System Disruptor / The Anomaly Seeker / The Cultural Reformer / The Data Ghost

Transfer Rule: Sustained anomaly engagement → Tier IV. Reinforcement of League authority → Tier II. Radical destabilization → Tier IV.

### Tier IV — Escalation Containment Layer
**Purpose:** Expose destabilizers to volatile conditions.

Characteristics:
- Biomes exhibit visible instability
- Mutation drift significant
- League fractured or militarized
- Relay Nodes active but unstable
- Environmental audio artifacts persistent

Player Endings: The Catalyst / The Fracture Harbinger / The System Exploiter / The Failed Reformer

Transfer Rule: Stabilized under Tier IV stress → Remain Tier IV. Containment threshold exceeded → Tier V. Player abandons anomaly pursuit → Tier III.

### Tier V — Valhalla Layer (Containment Apex)
**Purpose:** Test extreme anomaly variables under maximum stress.

Characteristics:
- High mutation baseline
- Aggressive League structures or dissolved League entirely
- Relay Nodes openly unstable
- Environmental distortions constant
- Some NPCs exhibit near-awareness

**This is not freedom. This is refinement.**

Player Endings: The Apex Variable / The Containment Breaker / The Recursive Subject / The Data Ascendant / The Archive Reset

Tier V does NOT destroy PROJECT HUNDRED. Instead:
- Island flagged for archival
- Player marked as High-Impact Variable
- Simulation parameters recomputed

Post-Tier V Outcomes:
1. Archive Transfer (new Tier I seed with subtle memory echoes)
2. Loop Recognition (player notices pattern across seeds)
3. Recursive Escalation (return to Tier V variant island)

## 24. New Game Plus (Hidden Escalation System)

Tier transfer is implemented through a familiar surface mechanic: "New Game+"

This is not traditional stat carryover. It is systemic carryover.

### 24.1 Surface Presentation

After completing an island, player sees:
- Ending Title
- Island Summary
- Option: "Begin New Expedition"
- Option: "Reset Simulation"

"Begin New Expedition" appears ordinary. It does not mention tiers. It internally computes the next containment tier.

### 24.2 What Carries Over (Subtle, Not Obvious)

Player does NOT carry: Neikos / Items / League Rank / Stats

System carries:
1. Behavioral Profile Signature
2. Anomaly Engagement History
3. Ecological Disruption Pattern
4. Dominance vs Harmony Bias

These modify:
- Mutation baseline
- League cultural tone
- Early anomaly visibility
- NPC suspicion levels
- Relay Node accessibility timing

### 24.3 Hidden Memory Echo System

Across tiers, the system may introduce:
- Recurring Neiko acoustic motifs
- Familiar biome distortions
- Subtle déjà vu dialogue lines

Not explicit memory. Statistical recurrence. Creates the sensation of continuity without breaking containment.

### 24.4 Reset Simulation Option

"Reset Simulation" clears behavioral carryover.
Produces Tier I baseline island, no systemic escalation, no echo artifacts.
This preserves player agency.

### 24.5 Long-Term Deep Dive Reward

Only players completing multiple tiers will detect:
- Escalation curve
- Structural similarities
- Increasing anomaly density
- Containment intensity

No UI confirms tier progression. Recognition is experiential.

**New Game+ is the camouflage. Tier escalation is the truth.**

## 27. Resolution Philosophy

- At least 2 Primary Mysteries reach partial resolution
- At least 1 Mystery remains unresolved
- Unresolved mysteries may escalate across tiers in later seeds

This prevents narrative closure from feeling artificial.
